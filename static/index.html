First transcription takes a moment

The first time you transcribe, Sonotype downloads the speech model (~500 MB) and caches it on your machine. Your Library and setup state are stored locally too, so they remain available when you reopen the app.

Try a short 5–10 second recording to get started. Once that finishes, you’re all set.
