GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Does my audio leave my computer?

No. Sonotype processes recordings and imported files locally. If you use the YouTube source, it downloads the permitted media locally before transcription; the transcript still stays on your machine.

Is Sonotype really free?

Yes. Sonotype is an open-source desktop utility with no account or subscription requirement. See the repository for the MIT license and source code.

What can I transcribe?

Record from your microphone or import common audio and video formats including MP3, WAV, M4A, MP4, MOV, and WebM. You can also prepare a permitted YouTube URL.

What happens the first time I open it?

Sonotype downloads the small.en speech model once, then caches it locally. A short 5–10 second test recording is the easiest way to get set up.
